# Lab Seminar: Descriptive Statistics

Snapshot: 19 October 2026.

## Counting Rules

All 17 loaded recordings are included. Main trials only; practice and session events are excluded. SOL = annotated acoustic speech onset minus S200, in milliseconds. Eligible trials have nonblank saved transcription, exactly one valid speech onset, and are not marked Unsuitable. Need help remains included. No SOL cutoff, outlier trimming, participant exclusion, or EEG artifact rejection was applied.

Tr ERG means marked Ergative. Tr zero (Tr ∅) means not marked Ergative; a missing saved decision is not independent confirmation that case marking was absent.

## Overall

- Participants: 17; recorded main trials: 2040.
- Transcribed: 2040; valid single onset: 2038; eligible: 1789.
- Unsuitable: 251; pending annotations among non-Unsuitable trials: 0.
- Need help: 156 / 2040 main trials (7.6%); 139 / 1789 eligible trials (7.8%). Need help and Unsuitable overlap on 17 trials.
- Eligible categories: Itr 858; Tr ERG 440; Tr ∅ 491.
- Trial-weighted SOL: mean 1354.1 ms; median 1281.5 ms; SD 419.4 ms.
- SOL middle 50%: 1068.7-1541.4 ms; range 699.4-4654.8 ms.
- Equal-weight mean of participant mean SOLs: 1362.0 ms.

SD above describes trials, not uncertainty of the group mean. No inferential test or Gaussian assumption is used.
